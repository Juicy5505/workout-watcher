    Tuning and tips
    Sensitivity: raise it if reps are missed, lower it if extra reps appear.
    • Each person must stay inside their lane. Lines on the video mark the lanes.
    • Counting needs each person's shoulders, hips and legs in view. A lane that can't see someone turns purple and says what's missing.
    • Each person moves to the next ticked movement on their own about 2.5 s after reaching its target.
    • Pushups: shoulder height (counting pauses while standing). Situps: shoulders above hips. Squats: hips above knees. Lunges: hips above ankles. Jumping jacks and overhead press: wrists above shoulders. Bicep curls: wrists above elbows (both arms together).
    • Floor movements need the whole body in view; curls and press only need the arms and shoulders.
    • Press F or "Big screen" so counts can be read from the floor.
    • Type into any count to correct it, or use −1 / +1.
